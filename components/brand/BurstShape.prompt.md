Oversized wobbly starburst — a scaled-up sibling of the sparkle, used as a bold background shape behind cutout photos or oversized headline type in playful, collage-style carousel slides.

```jsx
<BurstShape tone="lime" size={340} points={12} rotation={-8} />
```

Layer a cutout/transparent-background photo (a prop, a product, a hand) on top of it for a Y2K-collage look — keep colors within the Stigma palette (never the yellow/green of unrelated references).
