Small uppercase pill label — spaced-out caps like the logo's "Saloon & Nails Spa" descriptor. Use for service categories, status tags, story-highlight labels.

```jsx
<Badge tone="magenta">Color fantasía</Badge>
<Badge tone="charcoal" variant="outline">Nuevo</Badge>
```

Tones map to the accent palette (lemon/lilac/magenta/lime) plus charcoal for neutral tags. `solid` fills the pill; `outline` is a thin ring for lower-emphasis tags.
