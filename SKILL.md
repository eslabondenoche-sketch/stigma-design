Key things to remember about Stigma:
- Beauty salon/nail spa in Bogotá; content-led brand built primarily for Instagram + TikTok carousels about hair color, transformations, and inclusive beauty.
- Voice: warm, direct, honest, inclusive, tuteo, sparkle ✦ as signature sign-off. Never shame-adjacent words ("arreglar", "defecto", "normal").
- Palette: charcoal #1E1E1E + lemon #F8F058 as the 60/30 base; lilac/magenta/lime as 10% accents. Never lemon/lime as small text on white.
- Type: Playfair Display (serif, headlines) + Poppins (everything else).
- Signature motifs: the ✦ sparkle and the scalloped/wavy emblem frame.
